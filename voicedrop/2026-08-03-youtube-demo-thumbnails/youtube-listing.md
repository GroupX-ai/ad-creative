## Title

```
How to Send Ringless Voicemails: Full VoiceDrop 2.0 Setup
```

57 characters, so it survives truncation in search results. Front-loads the exact phrase people
search, names the version so it does not get confused with the 2024 videos already on the channel,
and matches the `/how-to-send-ringless-voicemail/` blog page.

Alternates considered:

- `VoiceDrop 2.0: Launch a Ringless Voicemail Campaign in Minutes` (61 chars) — closest to Brian's own
  Loom title, better if the video is mostly sent by sales rather than found by search, weaker for SEO.
- `Clone Your Voice and Send 100,000 Voicemails a Day` (49 chars) — the Meta hook ported over, grounded
  in Brian at 7:49. Highest click appeal, lowest search pickup. Worth testing as a second cut.
