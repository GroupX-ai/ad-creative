# VoiceDrop 2026-08-03 disruptive batch

**29 concepts, 52 assets, deliberately weird.** Robby asked to go wild for paid: this batch tests *style* as the variable while holding the claims constant. Every headline, subheadline and CTA is verbatim from the approved copy bank in `VoiceDrop-Marketing/AD-CREATIVE-PLAYBOOK.md` (or the site component named per concept). No invented stats, no third-party logos, no fake badges.

Generated with fal `openai/gpt-image-2`, quality high, ~$0.20/render (57 renders including 5 re-rolls, ~$11.40). Square 1024x1024 (Meta feed 1:1), vertical 1080x1920 (Stories/Reels 9:16 — an untested placement for this account), landscape 1200x624 (Google Display / Meta link ads 1.91:1).

## Two waves

**Wave 1 (c10-c28, 31 assets).** Nineteen visual territories the account has never run, against the format its data already likes. The 2026-08-03 billboard batch (c6-c9) is the control: Billboard Ads runs 3.9-4.0% CTR at $0.93-1.05 CPC over 90 days, while the product-shot Banner Test was killed 2026-08-03 with every ad under 2% CTR on $160.67.

**Wave 2 (c29-c38, 21 assets).** Robby picked four from wave 1 and called **c21, the whiteboard photo, "the best by far"** (the others: c10 hazard tape, c19 VHS, c22 split-screen). The hypothesis wave 2 tests is that c21 wins because it *reads as a real photograph in the feed rather than an advertisement*, not because it is literally a whiteboard. So wave 2 does two things:

1. Re-runs the winning whiteboard treatment against the other approved angles (c29-c33): callback proof, obsolete, voice clone, offer, and one caught mid-write with a hand in frame.
2. Tests the same insight on other physical media (c34-c38): yellow legal pad, sticky notes on a monitor, cafe napkin, torn cardboard, corkboard pin-up.

It also fills the placements Robby's four picks were missing, so each is now upload-ready in every format.

## Claim-safety notes

- **c27 and c28 are destination-locked.** "23% average callback rate" only exists on `/for-business-owners` and "callbacks start about 5 hours later" only on `/callbacks`. These two ads must point at those landers. Sending them to the homepage breaks the claims-match rule.
- **9%+ always ships with the Trustpilot attribution line** (c13, c25, c29, c38). Never mix 9%+ and 23% in one asset or one ad set.
- **c17 crops "1,000,000" off both frame edges on purpose** (scale device). The full, correctly punctuated figure is spelled out in the headline directly beneath it in every c17 asset.
- **c24 and c26 render an approximated VoiceDrop logo glyph.** The image model invents a mark; the real wordmark is `public/uploads/2024/01/VoiceDrop-Logo-Light.webp`. Composite the real file if either goes to a big-spend placement.
- **c11 landscape has tight edge margins.** Text is intact but close to the frame; prefer the square or vertical for that concept where a platform may crop.

## Suggested test split

Kill rules from the creative engine still apply: frequency >2.5 or CTR <2% after ~1,000 impressions = paused; judge on CTR/CPC after 2-3 days; winners get duplicated into the core campaigns, never left in a test campaign.

1. **The native/anti-design test (the real experiment):** c21, c29, c30, c33, c34, c35, c36, c37, c38 as one ad set. If organic-looking creative beats the polished billboards, that is a bigger finding than any single winner, and it is cheap to keep producing.
2. **Billboard Ads (Targeted - Sales):** c10 hazard, c19 VHS, plus c18 graffiti and c12 ransom note as the loudest pattern interrupts.
3. **Core Prospecting (Broad):** c11 brutalist, c13 chrome, c17 scale, c22 split-screen.
4. **SMS - Banner Ads:** c14 phone overflow, c26 offer, c32 whiteboard offer. This campaign needs variants most (Ad-13 carries it at $135 CPM, frequency 2.7).
5. **New Stories/Reels ad set, verticals:** c21, c29, c30, c33, c34, c35 lead; c10, c11, c14, c17, c18, c19, c22, c26 as the polished counterweight.
6. **Google Display, landscapes:** c10, c11, c13, c17, c19, c20, c21, c22, c24.
7. **Lander-locked, separate ad sets:** c27 to `/for-business-owners`, c28 to `/callbacks`.
