# VoiceDrop 2026-08-03 billboard batch

Four square (1024x1024) banners for Meta, generated per `resources/paid-ads-creative-playbook.md` (fal `openai/gpt-image-2`, quality high, ~$0.20/render). Built by the 2026-08-03 paid-acquisition audit session as the first weekly drop of the 3-5-variants-per-week cadence.

Why billboard style: it is the account's proven format. Billboard Ads runs 3.9-4.0% CTR at $0.93-1.05 CPC over 90 days, while the 2026-07-27 banner test (product-shot style, phone visuals) failed with every ad under 2% CTR after 7 days ($160.67 spent, test killed 2026-08-03). This batch re-cuts the approved claims as typography-led billboards.

All claims verbatim from the approved copy bank in `VoiceDrop-Marketing/AD-CREATIVE-PLAYBOOK.md`. Reviewed at full size: exact spelling, no invented stats or badges, brand colors correct (`#090714` bg, `#9746ff` purple, gold CTA `#ffd874` to `#ffb01f`), CTA "Get Free Access".
