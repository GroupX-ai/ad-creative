| `emailchaser-c12` | Leaked a stage direction into the read: "It will remain there, **its tone unchanged**, for the duration of your career." | Left as-is. It is three stray words in an otherwise clean take and still parses as a sentence. Re-roll if it bothers you ($6.39). |

**A false alarm worth recording:** `voicedrop-c40` first transcribed as "the North American cold
**baller**". A second pass with word timings returned "cold **caller**", correct. Nothing was
changed. This is exactly why a proper noun gets cross-checked before being called wrong.

## Two new prompt rules this batch taught

1. **Never write action as a staccato word list.** "pick, glance, flick" reads to the model as
   dialogue. Describe the action in full sentences instead.
2. **Add an explicit speech rule to every prompt.** Name the only lines that may be spoken and
   forbid narration, counting, muttering and reading the stage directions. The model improvises
   into silence otherwise, and what it improvises is often word salad.

Both are now in the vault playbook.
