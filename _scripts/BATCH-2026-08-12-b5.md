# Batch 5 — 2026-08-12 — research-driven UGC ads (5 clips, ~$49.3)

Robby's brief: "do extensive research on ad scripts for B2B SaaS that convert. Then use
those to come up with killer ideas for video ads (15-60 seconds). Create a few for me
(max budget is $100 for everything together)."

## How this batch was made

1. **Research sweep (7 agents).** Web research on direct-response script frameworks,
   hooks, UGC/founder formats and CTA framing, plus a live Meta Ads Library pull:
   ~2,000 ads across big B2B SaaS advertisers (ClickUp, monday.com, Grammarly, Notion,
   Rippling, Motion) and ~250 ads across direct competitors (Instantly, Smartlead,
   lemlist, Apollo, Earlybird, ScaledMail, Prospeo, ringless-voicemail and validation
   players). Longevity = the public conversion proxy. Full output:
   `RESEARCH-2026-08-12-b2b-scripts-synthesis.md`.
2. **Own-account evidence.** Best VoiceDrop ads at volume are first-person claims with
   a number or an absence ("I Sent 5000 Voicemails" 3.38% CTR, "19-Year-Old Intern"
   3.32%, "Haven't Made A Cold Call In A Year" $581 at 2.04%); EC C17, the batch-3
   Seedance UGC ad, is Emailchaser's best CTR at volume (3.06% on 1,831 impressions).
3. **Two verified competitor gaps drove the angles.** Nobody in ~250 active cold-email
   competitor ads claims the primary inbox (Emailchaser's exact pitch, zero Meta
   competition), and ringless voicemail is an empty auction with no category video.
4. **Nine candidate scripts, three-judge panel** (hook strength, claim safety, render
   safety). Five survived; four were cut for angle collisions with live ads, a banned
   callback-rate hook, or an abstract opener. Every judge line-fix was applied.

## QA results

- All 5 transcribed verbatim against script (scribe-v2), including both VoiceDrop URLs
  and all three Emailchaser closes.
- **c49 roll 1 failed:** the phone rendered a caller label plausibly reading
  "Voicedrop", the banned inverted mechanic (c22/c45 class). Re-rolled with the
  hardened PHONE RULE (screen = two circles on empty black, exhaustively stated in
  PLACE and the rule). Roll 2 rendered a perfectly clean screen in every frame.
- **c20 roll 1 failed:** spoke "Email Chaper" (confirmed scribe-v2 + Whisper before
  calling it real). Re-rolled with a rhyme anchor added to the phonetic rule
  ("chaser rhymes exactly with racer"). Roll 2 correct.
- c48 note: the tiny caller line on the stairwell phone rendered as illegible
  soft-focus garble ~30px wide. No brand reading, correct mechanic, invisible at feed
  size. Shipped; thumbnail should avoid a phone close-up.
