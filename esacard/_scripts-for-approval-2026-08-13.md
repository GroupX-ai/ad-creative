# ESA Card video scripts, for approval before anything is rendered

Nothing in this document has been generated. Standing rule from Robby, 2026-08-13:
**never render a video ad without sending the script first.**

## What was wrong with the last batch

1. **The CTA was in a human being's mouth.** Real people do not say "Emotional support
   animal card, thirty-nine dollars" to a camera. The product name and the price belong on
   an end card. Dialogue is dialogue.
2. **The story was disconnected from the product.** A man cuddling an old dog at golden hour
   is a nice fifteen seconds of film, but nothing in it involved the thing being sold, so the
   last line arrived out of nowhere.

## The four rules these scripts follow

1. **Every script is a real situation where having the card changed something.** If the card
   could be removed from the scene without changing the story, the script is cut.
2. **The phrase "emotional support animal card" is spoken in every script**, and in four of
   the five it is the *other* person who asks for it by name. That is the natural way to say
   it out loud, and it carries a useful implication: this is a normal thing to be asked for.
3. **Nobody speaks the price or the URL.** That is end card only.
4. **The improvement is the point.** Each one has a before and an after, said the way a
   person would mention it, not the way an advertiser would.

The honest benefit all five are built on: **you stop having to explain your animal to
strangers.** It removes a recurring, low-grade, slightly humiliating friction. That is what
the card actually does, it is what the marketing site already claims ("makes everyday moments
easier at leasing offices, with building staff, and at pet-friendly hotels"), and it is worth
$39 to a lot of people.

Every script ends on the same still end card, with no voice over it:

> **Emotional support animal certificate and ID card**
> **$39, one time · esacard.com**

Spoken word counts are 34–38, which is the budget that worked for the fifteen-second clips
this account has already shipped.

## Notes on production

- **Two-hander dialogue is new for us.** Every previous Seedance ad on this account was one
  person talking to a phone. Scripts 1, 2 and 5 need a second speaker and a reply. The model
  can do it, but it has not been asked to here before, so if any renders badly the fallback is
  to keep the scene and put the other person's line off camera.
- **"Emotional support animal card" is five words in a row and the model has mispronounced
  brand names before.** The prompt will carry an explicit rule to say the phrase in full, and
  every clip gets transcribed on two engines before delivery, the same check that caught
  "Email Chaper" on a previous batch.
- **The card stays blank** under the existing prop rule: white rectangle, small photo of the
  animal, three grey lines, nothing else. No seal, no crest, no readable text.
- **The end card is added in post**, not rendered, so the price and the URL are always correct
  and never garbled by the model.
- Cost is about **$6.94 per 15-second clip** at 720p.
